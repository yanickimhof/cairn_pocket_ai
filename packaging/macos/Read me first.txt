Cairn: your Pocket, your computer, your data.

1. Double-click "Install Cairn". A Terminal window opens and asks which models to install; press Enter to take the advised ones.
2. When it is done, Cairn opens in your browser. Next time, open Cairn from the Cairn folder in your home folder.

If macOS says it cannot check "Install Cairn":
  - macOS 15 and later: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about "Install Cairn" and press "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  - macOS 14: Control-click "Install Cairn", choose Open, then Open again.
The installer is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks once. Cairn is open source, and you can read what the installer does: https://github.com/yanickimhof/cairn

Nothing leaves your computer. To uninstall, delete the Cairn folder in your home folder.
