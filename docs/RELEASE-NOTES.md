# Release notes

## Coming in 0.5

**Who said what, across all your notes.**
Rename, merge and review speakers once, and let Cairn recognise voices if you want it to.

**Ask your notes.**
Ask a question, get an answer with its sources, all on your Mac.

**A vocabulary that learns.**
Cairn lists the words it doubts, you give the spelling, and every note is updated.

## 0.4

**Find anything, in a second.**
Search every word of every note, accents ignored, your words highlighted. `#orion` narrows it to a tag.

**Hear what was said.**
Tap a time stamp in the transcript and the recording plays from that line. Checking a doubtful word takes seconds.

**All your actions, one list.**
Every action from every note, by person, newest first. Tick them, correct them, copy them.

**The follow-up email, written.**
One tap drafts a short email from the note, in its language, ready for your mail app. Cairn sends nothing.

**Your names, spelled right, in context.**
Several spellings can point to one name, even only near the right words. One tap updates the notes that need it: all of them, a choice, or a period.

**Notes in your own format.**
Write a template once, a name and an instruction, and every note follows it, sections included. A star makes it the default.

**Tags, not folders.**
Free words to organize your notes, a filter above the list, tagging by the dozen, and suggestions drawn from your own tags.

**Fresher engine.**
The libraries behind transcription are rebuilt for macOS 14 and later, closing five security advisories. A second copy can now run beside the first for testing.

## 0.3

**Your names, spelled right.**
Teach Cairn the names and places it gets wrong. Every transcript and summary then spells them your way.

**Speakers, 4.5 times faster.**
Cairn now uses your Mac's graphics chip to tell who is speaking. Same result, a fraction of the time.

**Fix who said what.**
A voice got the wrong label? Redo the speakers of any note and give each one a name.

**It keeps going without you.**
With automatic processing on, Cairn starts as soon as your recordings are copied, keeps your Mac awake, and tries again if something fails. Your notes are ready when you are.

**Easy on your Mac.**
Memory is freed between steps, so even a 16 GB Mac stays quick.

**Always honest.**
Models and About show exactly what is installed on your Mac, sizes and versions included.

## 0.2

**Who said what.**
Cairn tells speakers apart, entirely on your Mac.

**Your Pocket at a glance.**
Add your own session key to see its firmware, state and storage, read only.

**Always the latest.**
The install command now installs the newest release.

## 0.1

**Install in one line.**
One command sets up everything on macOS or Windows, then checks that it works.

**Plug in your Pocket.**
Recordings are copied and verified, and pick up where they stopped if you unplug.

**Notes that get to the point.**
A title, a summary, the actions and the key points, in the format that fits: meeting, call, presentation or idea.

**Know how far to trust it.**
Every note shows how close it is to what was said.

**Your language, your level of detail.**
Choose for each recording, and redo a note any time.

**Share in one click.**
Copy it, or export to Markdown or HTML.

**You stay in control.**
Process, skip or stop any recording. Delete once and it is gone from the Pocket and your Mac.

**Light to install, powerful on demand.**
Start small, add bigger models when you want them, remove them whenever you like.

**Private, and provably.**
No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Cairn checks every day that nothing leaves your Mac.

**Always there.**
Runs quietly in the background, in English or French.
